Founding

Kalium was founded in 2026 by Camille Canon and Caroline Ciaramitaro, who spent a decade apiece inside the machinery of capital and came out convinced it could do one thing more.

She writes the white paper, the essays, and Loaded, and hosts the gatherings.

Caroline Ciaramito
