Kalium

Abstract

Dynamic equity creates private returns and public goods.

Kalium is an investment platform piloting a new kind of investment. It works like any other — same terms, same rigor, alongside conventional capital — with one addition: an agreed cap on our financial return. Above the cap, surplus value builds public goods through the company itself, defined with the founder at the start. Below it, nothing is different.

Private returns, capped. Public goods, built in.

Premise

Capitalism works.

It is the best machine anyone has ever built for turning ideas into value. We are not here to fix it. We are here because of what we keep doing with it.

Sequence

Value → Private Wealth · 01–02

A company creates value. The value flows out. It becomes private wealth.

Sequence, Cont.

Return · 01–03

Years later — sometimes decades — some of it travels back as philanthropy, to repair problems the same companies were best positioned to prevent. We pay twice: once to extract, once to repair.

Reservoir

UNBOUND · $326B

Right now, more than $326 billion sits parked in donor-advised funds — committed to the public, under no obligation ever to move. The intention is real. The structure is the problem.

Instruction

The corporation is the most effective public-goods-producing machine we have. It simply runs on a single instruction — return value to its owners. That instruction is not a law of nature. It is a term in an agreement. And terms can change.

Amendment

So we changed one term. One.

Mechanism

Fig. A–C

Dynamic equity creates private returns and public goods. It works like any investment — the same rooms, the same rigor, side by side with conventional capital. One addition: an agreed cap on our financial return. Below the cap, nothing is different. Above it, the surplus builds public goods through the company itself defined together with the founder, at the start, in writing.

Structure

One Atom · Nothing External

Nothing here is external. Everything orbits the mission. The returns fills toward the cap and closes and what exceeds it doesn't leave. It orbits.

Identity is the nucleus. Behavior is the outer shell.

SELECTION

CAPITAL MEANING

The founder chooses what the surplus builds. The asset holder chooses what their capital means.

SURPLUS
  1. 01
    New research for everyone A pharmaceutical company's R&D pipeline as a net-new public good.
  2. 02
    A smaller footprint Cutting the carbon intensity of the company's own operations.
  3. 03
    Upside shared with the people who built it Workers and communities who help create the value share in it (employee liquidity pool, community trust).
  4. 04
    Essential things kept affordable Lowering the cost of goods and services for the people who depend on them.
  5. 05
    A model or dataset that stays open A nonprofit holds or licenses the company's IP so an acquirer can't enclose it (open-data trusts and patent pledges as precedent).
  6. 06
    A promise welded to the asset Covenants that bind the acquirer: the price stays below a set threshold, access survives the sale.
  7. 07
    Paying for an outcome, not an institution Surplus funds a commitment that pulls a specific good into existence, proven in vaccine and essential-medicine markets (advance market commitment).
  8. 08
    A purpose trust Surplus lands in a restricted vehicle built to fund one defined public benefit.
  9. 09
    Seeding the next one Surplus recycles into the next generation of public-goods companies; each exit funds more of the same ("self-propagating infrastructure").

Classification

This is not impact investing. It is not concessionary. Nothing here asks a founder to build differently, or an investor to accept less rigor. Watch the returns.

Pilot

TEMPLATES · PUBLIC

We are piloting now, at early stage, and publishing the documents as we go: the templates, the governance, the lessons. Copy us. Being copied is the point.

Horizon

STANDARD, NOT SPECIAL

In five years this should not be a movement. It should be a checkbox at the largest asset managers in the world. The goal is not to be special. It is to be standard.
