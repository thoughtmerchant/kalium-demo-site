Abstract

Dynamic equity
creates private
returns and
public goods.

Kalium is an investment platform piloting a new kind of investment. It works like any other — same terms, same rigor, alongside conventional capital — with one addition: an agreed cap on our financial return. Above the cap, surplus value builds public goods through the company itself, defined with the founder at the start. Below it, nothing is different.

Private returns, capped. Public goods, built in.
